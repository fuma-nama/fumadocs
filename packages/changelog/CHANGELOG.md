## @fumadocs/changelog@0.1.0

### New package: `@fumadocs/changelog`

- `ChangelogEntry` describes an entry of your changelog, like a release.
- `githubReleases()` from `@fumadocs/changelog/github` gets a page of the published releases of a GitHub repository.
- `npx @fumadocs/cli add changelog` installs a timeline to render the entries.
